Fisting Fury

A free, ad-free, single-player arcade city brawler by Green Gary Games.

Support

Email greengarygames@gmail.com with your device model, game version and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Move with the left stick and drag the open right side to aim. Use the contextual attack button to hit, stomp or use an equipped special. Specials unlock at 1,000 points per character. Use EQUIP/HOLSTER to switch weapons and fists. Use the interaction button near vehicles or building entrances. Options includes music and No Pigs settings.

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

The native Android and iPhone game does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to Green Gary Games. It contains no advertising, in-app purchases, behavioral analytics, online chat or cloud saves. Progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Removing the game's local data removes that saved progress, subject to your device's backup settings.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address.

Apple, Google and your device provider may process store, download, purchase-history or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process standard website access information. These services are separate from the game's local save data.

The game contains strong language, stylized violence, blood and dismemberment and is intended for an adult audience. It is not designed for children.

Policy changes will be published on this page. Contact: greengarygames@gmail.com.
