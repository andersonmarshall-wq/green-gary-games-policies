Fingering Frenzy privacy policy

Green Gary Games · October 20, 2026

Fingering Frenzy is a free piano tile game. The game does not ask for an account and does not contain advertising, in-app purchases, or an app analytics service.

Data on your device

The game saves scores and settings locally. Green Gary Games does not receive these records. You can remove them by clearing the game's app data or uninstalling it. Browser storage can be removed through your browser settings.

Permissions and sharing

The game does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or your personal files. It does not upload gameplay records or share them with advertisers.

Stores and web hosting

Apple, Google, and itch.io may process information when you use their stores, testing services, or websites under their own privacy policies. The browser edition downloads game files from its host; that host may receive ordinary web request information such as your IP address and browser details. Those platform functions are separate from the local scores and settings stored by the game.

Young players

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Contact and updates

Questions: greengarygames@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change.
