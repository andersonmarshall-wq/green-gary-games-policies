Fingering Frenzy Support

A free, ad-free piano rhythm game by Green Gary Games.

Get help

Email greengarygames@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, game version, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

How to play

Choose a track, then tap its lowest tile to begin. Tap single notes, keep a finger on held notes, and use two fingers for chords. Try practice mode for a slower pace or Encore for an ongoing challenge. Pause before leaving a song.

Sound and timing

Check the game's mute setting and your device volume. Bluetooth headphones can add audio delay; try the device speaker or wired headphones if timing feels late.

Progress and privacy

The game needs no account. Scores and settings are saved on your device, with no cloud sync. Removing the app may remove this progress. The game has no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions.
